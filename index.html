§II The Firm

A firm whose only mandate is yours.

Ninth Protocol is a private procurement firm registered in Estonia, acting for ultra-high-net-worth individuals and family offices globally. We source what cannot be found, acquire what is not for sale, and protect the only asset that does not renew.

  1. 01

    Conflict-Free

    We earn no commissions, rebates, or referral fees from any third party. The client is our only counterparty.

  2. 02

    Limited Roster

    A deliberately small number of clients. One point of contact with authority to act. No account-manager triage.

  3. 03

    Held in Confidence

    We do not name references, confirm engagements, or discuss our clients. Discretion enforced beyond the mandate.

§III The Difference

The model precedes everything.

Most concierge and procurement firms operate on commission. Their advice is not independent — it is incentivised. We chose the other way, entirely.

01

The Fiduciary Standard

We act for the principal — not the supplier. Fees are agreed directly with the client before any mandate commences. No undisclosed margins. No third-party kickbacks. No conflict of interest, ever.

A fiduciary relationship cannot exist alongside a commission structure.
02

One Principal at a Time

You are not allocated to a queue or a team. You have a single point of contact with full authority to act — and the discretion to do so without involving anyone who does not need to know.

Complex mandates require full attention. Full attention requires constraint.
03

Counsel, Not Just Execution

We recommend against a purchase when the evidence does not support it. We tell you when a price is wrong, when provenance is uncertain, when a better option exists in a market you do not currently see.

That counsel is what you are retaining. Not execution alone — judgement.
04

Discretion by Structure

Discretion is not a promise made in copy. It is enforced — through limited documentation, controlled access, and a standing non-disclosure that extends beyond the mandate's termination.

We understand what is at stake when the wrong person knows the right detail.

What we are retained to do.

Four disciplines. One standard. Each mandate is direct, finite, and conflict-free.

01

Automotive & Aviation

Off-market hypercars. JDM acquisitions. Coachbuilt commissions. Private aircraft acquisition and discreet charter management. We source what is not publicly available — and verify provenance, pricing, and condition before you commit.

  • Provenance verification
  • Cross-border registration
  • Aircraft charter management
02

Rare Horology & Collectibles

Patek Philippe. Audemars Piguet. Richard Mille. Art, manuscripts, instruments, and other assets where the constraint is not budget but access. We acquire through relationships, not auction sites.

  • Discreet transfer
03

Experiences & Access

Invitation-only events. Itineraries that cannot be booked. Introductions that cannot be requested. We open doors that are not advertised, in capitals where standing is not for sale.

  • Itinerary design
  • Standing introductions

Logistics & Project Direction

Bespoke vehicle builds. Frame-off restorations. White-glove transport of high-value assets across borders. Multi-stage projects where the cost of error in time, money, or condition is not acceptable.
