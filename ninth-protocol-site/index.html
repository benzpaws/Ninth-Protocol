Article III · The Model

A firm whose only mandate is yours.

01

The Fiduciary Standard

Most procurement firms operate on commission. Their advice is not independent — it is incentivised.

Ninth Protocol accepts no commissions, rebates, or referral fees from any third party. Our fee is agreed directly with the client. That is the complete arrangement.

A fiduciary relationship cannot exist alongside a commission structure. We chose the former, entirely.

02

One Principal

We serve a deliberately limited number of clients. Complex mandates require full attention. Full attention requires constraint.

You are not allocated to an account manager. You have a single point of contact — with the authority to act, and the discretion to do so without involving parties who do not need to know.

03

Conflict-Free by Design

We hold no preferred supplier relationships, no inventory positions, and no financial interest in any outcome other than the result you wanted.

We will recommend against a purchase when the evidence does not support it. We will tell you when a price is wrong, when provenance is uncertain, when a better option exists elsewhere.

That counsel is what you are retaining. Not execution alone — judgement.

04

Discretion as Discipline

We do not discuss our clients. We do not confirm engagements. We do not name references.

Discretion at this level is not a promise made in copy. It is enforced — through limited documentation, controlled access, and a standing non-disclosure that extends beyond the mandate.

"When you engage Ninth Protocol, there is no question of whose interests are being served."

— Operating Principle 9.1

03

Invitation-only events. Itineraries that cannot be booked. Introductions that cannot be requested. We open the door.

04

Logistics & Project Direction

Bespoke builds. Restorations. Cross-border transport of high-value assets. White-glove management from brief to completion.

Every mandate operates under the same terms. One client. One mandate. Zero conflicts.

The Standard

The standard does not vary by mandate size.

Whether the assignment is a single watch or a private aircraft transaction, the protocol is the same.

Conflict-free. Discreet. Accountable to one principal only.
