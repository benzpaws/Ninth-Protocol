What We Are

A firm whose only mandate is yours.

Ninth Protocol is a private procurement firm. We source and secure — vehicles, timepieces, aviation, and extraordinary experiences — on behalf of individuals who expect the matter resolved, not managed.

We hold no inventory. We carry no supplier affiliations. We earn no commissions. Our fee comes from one source: the client. That arrangement is not incidental — it is the entire point.

When you retain Ninth Protocol, you retain a firm whose only mandate is yours.

The Fiduciary Standard

The model precedes everything.

Most procurement and concierge firms operate on commission. They are compensated, in part, by the suppliers they recommend to you. In that structure, the advice you receive is not independent — it is incentivised.

Ninth Protocol does not accept commissions, rebates, or referral fees from any third party. Our fee is agreed directly with the client before any mandate commences. That is the complete arrangement.

This is not a differentiating feature. It is a precondition. A fiduciary relationship cannot exist alongside a commission structure. We chose the former, entirely.

When you engage Ninth Protocol, there is no question of whose interests are being served.
