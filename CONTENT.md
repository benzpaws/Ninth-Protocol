## Forbidden phrases (do not use anywhere)

These have been deliberately rejected in past brand work and must not reappear:

- "luxury" (we serve UHNW, not "luxury seekers")
- "exclusive" (cheapened by overuse in concierge marketing)
- "world-class" (always cheap)
- "bespoke" (overused, drained of meaning)
- "seamless" (SaaS-speak)
- "solutions" (jargon)
- "offerings" (jargon)
- "concierge" (we are not — we are a procurement firm)
- "curated" (overused)
- "leverage", "synergy", "ecosystem" (corporate noise)
- "trusted partner" / "your trusted" (cliché)
- "white-glove" — exception: explicitly allowed once in §III Logistics as a technical industry term, not a marketing phrase
- "elevate" / "elevating" (overused in lifestyle copy)
- Em-dashes are allowed, but never in marketing-style flourishes ("we don't just X — we Y")

## Tone of voice

- Declarative, not aspirational
- Present tense, active voice, second-person ("you") only where the client is being addressed directly
- Short sentences are good. Very short sentences are great.
- One thought per paragraph
- Periods. Not exclamation marks. Not ellipses for trailing-off effect.
- Italics for emphasis. Bold only in inline-label contexts ("**Tier I**...").
- The site speaks the way a private banker speaks: low, slow, certain.
